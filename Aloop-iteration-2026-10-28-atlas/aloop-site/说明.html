说明

本页集中说明 Aloop 的使用方式、第三方资源来源、许可信息与技术边界。许可状态仅按当前代码中能够识别的来源记录；发布前仍应逐项核对依赖版本、资源文件及上游许可文件。

网站使用

通过顶部「实验」进入实验模块，通过「产品」访问 EasySci 与 Smart Brain。首页大脑为品牌展示交互，使用自托管解剖模型的皮层网格采样生成粒子，支持指针旋转与点击粒子扩散；Smart Brain 属于独立产品模块（含探索图谱与学习讲解），其模型加载与交互状态与首页隔离，首页动画不会替代产品模型。部分功能依赖浏览器 WebGL；Three.js 通过 import map 从固定版本的 CDN 加载，模型与 Draco 解码器随站点自托管。

心理学实验与学习内容仅供研究/教学用途，不构成临床诊断或治疗建议。请在开展研究前完成适当的伦理审查、知情同意与数据保护安排。

首页大脑模型

模型与清单已随站点自托管在 assets/models/atlas/（brain.glb、manifest.json、draco/ 解码器），不再从 raw.githubusercontent.com 或 gstatic.com 加载；该域名在部分地区会被重置，曾导致首页与 Smart Brain 同时报「模型加载失败」。上游项目：itayinbarr/brainproject，上游原始模型：brain.glb；许可与校验和见 assets/models/atlas/ATTRIBUTION.md。

项目现有源码注释将解剖几何资源归因于 Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D / DBCLS，并标注 CC BY-SA 4.0；该标注应与实际下载版本中的元数据和上游许可文件再次核验。首页使用独立场景采样模型表面粒子，隐藏的是首页场景内的实体网格，不修改 Smart Brain 场景的材质或可见性。

Using the site

Open the experiments through Experiments and EasySci or Smart Brain through Products. The homepage brain is a brand interaction: its particles are sampled from the cortical mesh of the self-hosted anatomical model, with pointer rotation and click diffusion. Smart Brain is a separate product module (atlas exploration plus guided lessons); its model loading and interaction state stay isolated from the homepage, and the homepage animation never substitutes for the product model. Some features require WebGL; Three.js is loaded from a pinned CDN version through an import map, while the model and the Draco decoder are self-hosted.

Psychology experiments and learning material are for research and teaching only; they are not clinical diagnosis or treatment. Complete appropriate ethics review, informed consent and data-protection arrangements before running a study.

Homepage brain model

The model and manifest are self-hosted under assets/models/atlas/ (brain.glb, manifest.json and the draco/ decoder) instead of being fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com or gstatic.com; those hosts are reset in some regions, which previously made both the homepage and Smart Brain report a model load failure. Upstream project: itayinbarr/brainproject; original model: brain.glb. Licence and checksums: assets/models/atlas/ATTRIBUTION.md.

Existing source comments attribute the anatomical geometry to Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D / DBCLS under CC BY-SA 4.0; that attribution should be re-checked against the metadata of the downloaded version and the upstream licence files. The homepage samples surface particles in its own scene and only hides solid meshes inside that scene; it never mutates Smart Brain materials or visibility.
